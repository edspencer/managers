Answering a task: the options are real primary buttons (hover, focus ring, keyboard), and the "Wake the manager now" switch is replaced by two actions, **Answer** and **Answer & wake** — on an options task, each option's "& wake" half. Answer & wake names the trigger it fires when that is not `wake` ("Answer & wake (roadmap)") and is hidden, not disabled, when nothing can be woken. Every `Button` now shows a keyboard focus ring.
